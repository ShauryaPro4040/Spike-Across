SPIKE ACROSS GUIDE

INSTRUCTIONS

🎮 CONTROLS

A / D — Move left / right

Double-tap A / D — Dive and save

SPACE — Jump

Left-click / SPIKE — Attack or serve

S / BUMP — Receive the ball

E / SET — Pop the ball high

ESC — Pause / resume

🏐 HOW TO PLAY

1. Serve to start the rally.

2. Use a bump or set on your first touch.

3. Jump and time your spike on your next touch for a faster, flatter shot.

4. Move into position and dive to save low balls.

5. Send the ball over the net and make it land in the opponent’s court.

📜 RULES

• First to 7 points, win by 2.

• Each side gets up to 3 touches before sending it back.

• A first touch must be a bump, set, or dive-save — no spike.

• Ball on the floor on your side gives the opponent a point.

• Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard before playing.
